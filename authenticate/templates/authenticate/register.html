Privacy and Security

We take your privacy very seriously. We are committed to protecting your privacy and security. Why register with Chip'n Run?

Here at Chip'n Run we take golf seriously and aim to provide you with everything you need to run your golf society successfully.

Run your society

Register your members, create your competitions, run your Order Of Merit, track society handicaps, see who has most birdies, most bogies and the highest score on a hole.

Keep track of your own scores

Here at Chip'n Run we give you all the tools to record the scores of you and your society members.

Organise your away days and trips

Tired of keeping track of who you have asked, who has replied, who has paid in spreadsheets or on pieces of paper, then let our site take the hassle out of organising you golf day, weekend away or trip abroad.

Connect with others

Looking to meet up with like minded golfers or working away from home and fancy playing a new course with a local member, then connect with others via our site.

Get the inside scoop

Keep up to date with what your golf buddies are doing, where they are playing and what they are scoring. Sign up today and enjoy all these benefits

About ChipnRun

ChipnRun was developed after the need for a social, fun platform for recording, tracking and comparing scores was identified by a group of golf buddies. All involved with IT and marketing they had the necessary skills to get the system running the way they wanted it to be.
